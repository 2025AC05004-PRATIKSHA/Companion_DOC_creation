Session 9: Testing of Hypothesis Foundations

Master hypothesis testing, significance levels, p-values, and Z-test decisions.

1. Hypothesis Testing Principles

We start with a null hypothesis \(H_0\) and compute a test statistic. If the calculated statistic falls into the rejection region, we reject \(H_0\).

\[ Z = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu_0}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}} \]
