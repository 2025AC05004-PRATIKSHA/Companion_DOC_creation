ISM Session 14

Advanced Time Series: ARIMA, SARIMAX \& VAR

Prepared for Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP), BITS Pilani.

Explore advanced forecasting models: ARMA(p,q), integrated ARIMA(p,d,q), seasonal SARIMA, exogenous SARIMAX, and multivariate Vector Autoregression (VAR/VARMAX).

1. ARMA(p, q)

Combines $p$ autoregressive lags with $q$ moving average shock corrections.

2. SARIMAX

Layer differencing $d$, seasonal cycles $m$, and external predictor variables $X_t$.

3. VAR(p) Systems

Model multiple interdependent target series with cross-lag mutual feedback.

01 · Memory & Shocks

ARMA(1, 1) Numerical Forecast

ARMA(1,1) combines autoregressive parameter $\phi_1$ with moving average shock parameter $\theta_1$: $y_t = c + \phi_1 y_{t-1} + \epsilon_t + \theta_1 \epsilon_{t-1}$.

$$ \hat{y}_{101} = 3 + 0.5(10) + 0.4(2) = 8.8 $$

⚡ ARMA(1,1) Multi-Step Forecaster

Adjust residual shock $\epsilon_{100}$ to observe how MA terms only affect 1-step forecasts while AR terms persist.

02 · Differencing & Exogenous Inputs

SARIMAX Demand Forecasting

SARIMAX incorporates promotion flags $X_t$ with regression coefficient $\beta$ into the seasonal ARIMA model structure.

$$ \hat{y}_{101} = 4 + 0.6 y_{100} + \beta X_{101} $$

⚡ Promotion Flag Simulator

Toggle promotion flag $X_{101} = 1$ vs $0$ to see how exogenous marketing shifts demand baseline.

03 · Multivariate Interdependence

VAR(1) GDP \& Inflation System

VAR(1) models GDP growth $g_t$ and inflation $\pi_t$ simultaneously, capturing mutual feedback between economic series.

⚡ VAR(1) Cross-Lag Simulator

Adjust previous inflation $\pi_{99}$ to observe its direct feedback cross-impact on GDP growth $g_{100}$.

Session 14 Pocket Cheat-Sheet

ARMA(p, q)

Stationary model combining $p$ AR past memory terms and $q$ MA shock terms.

SARIMAX

Handles trends ($d$), seasonal cycles ($m$), and external driver variables ($X_t$).

VAR(p)

Multivariate system where each variable depends on lagged values of all variables.
