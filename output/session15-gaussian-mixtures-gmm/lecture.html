01 · Multimodal Density

Gaussian Mixture Formulation

GMMs combine $K$ Gaussian components with mixing proportions $\pi_k$ to model complex, overlapping data distributions.

$$ p(x) = \sum_{k=1}^K \pi_k \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_k, \sigma_k^2) $$

⚡ Bimodal Step Count Density Visualizer

Adjust component mean $\mu_2$ to watch active student step density separate from sedentary students.

02 · Soft Clustering

EM Algorithm Responsibilities

The E-step computes posterior membership probabilities $\gamma(z_k) = P(z_k=1 \mid x)$ via Bayes' theorem.

$$ \gamma(z_k) = \frac{\pi_k \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_k, \sigma_k^2)}{\sum_{j=1}^K \pi_j \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_j, \sigma_j^2)} $$

Slide observation value $x$ to inspect soft assignment probabilities across sedentary vs active clusters.

04 · Summary Card

Session 15 Pocket Cheat-Sheet

GMM Density

$p(x) = \sum \pi_k \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_k, \Sigma_k)$. Linear combination of Gaussians.

E-Step

Computes $\gamma(z_k) = P(z_k=1 \mid x)$. Soft cluster assignment.

M-Step

Updates $\mu_k, \Sigma_k, \pi_k$ weighted by current responsibilities.
