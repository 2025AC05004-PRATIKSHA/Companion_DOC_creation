01 · Variance Analysis

Two-Way ANOVA (Without Interaction)

Two-Way ANOVA evaluates the isolated effects of two distinct categorical variables on a continuous target variable at the same time.

Analogy. If you bake cakes, baking temperature (low, med, high) is row factor A and sugar brand is column factor B. Two-Way ANOVA calculates if temperature matters and if sugar brand matters without letting them mix up.
$$ SST = SSA + SSB + SSE $$

02 · Association Strength

Karl Pearson Correlation Coefficient

Correlation standardizes covariance by dividing by standard deviation product, producing unitless $r$ between $-1$ and $+1$.

$$ r = \frac{\sum (X-\bar{X})(Y-\bar{Y})}{\sqrt{\sum (X-\bar{X})^2 \sum (Y-\bar{Y})^2}} $$

03 · Trend Fitting

Simple Linear Regression

Regression calculates optimal slope $b$ and intercept $a$ minimizing squared residual error distances: $\hat{Y} = a + bX$.

$$ b = \frac{\sum (X-\bar{X})(Y-\bar{Y})}{\sum (X-\bar{X})^2}, \quad a = \bar{Y} - b\bar{X} $$

04 · Summary Card

Session 11A Pocket Cheat-Sheet

ANOVA F-Ratio

$F_A = MSA / MSE$, $F_B = MSB / MSE$. Tests row and column main effects.

Pearson $r$

$r = \text{Cov}(X,Y) / (s_X s_Y)$. Ranges $-1 \le r \le +1$.

Regression Line

$\hat{Y} = a + bX$. Minimizes total squared residual errors.
