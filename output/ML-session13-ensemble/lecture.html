1. Wisdom of Crowds & Ensemble Intuition

Combining predictions from multiple models reduces error. When weak models make independent errors, averaging them cancels out individual variance.

Intuition: A weak learner performs slightly better than random guessing (e.g. 55% accuracy). Combining 100 weak learners yields near-perfect predictions.

2. Bagging & Random Forests

Bootstrap Aggregation (Bagging) samples data with replacement. Random Forests add feature subsampling to decorrelate individual trees.

Feature Subsampling Formula:
\[ m = \sqrt{p} \]
For \(p\) total features, Random Forests select \(m = \sqrt{p}\) features at each node split.
