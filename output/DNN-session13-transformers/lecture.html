1. Intuition & Positional Encoding

Self-attention is an order-blind operation. To give words sequence order, we add sinusoidal positional encoding vectors to token embeddings.

0 (sin)

2. Layer Normalization

LayerNorm computes the mean and variance across feature dimensions for a single token independently of batch size.

Key Equation
$$\hat{x}_i = \frac{x_i - \mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2 + \epsilon}} \cdot \gamma + \beta$$

3. Scaled Dot-Product Attention

Queries $Q$, Keys $K$, and Values $V$ produce contextualized representations via scaled dot products.

4. Multi-Head Attention Lab

Multiple attention heads project tokens into different representation subspaces simultaneously.

5. Causal Masking (Decoder-Only)

Decoders use upper-triangular $-\infty$ masking to prevent tokens from attending to future words during autoregressive generation.

6. Cross-Attention Alignment

In sequence-to-sequence translation, decoder queries meet encoder keys and values to align translated words.

7. BERT & Model Taxonomy

Transformers split into Encoder-Only (BERT, bidirectional), Decoder-Only (GPT, causal), and Encoder-Decoder (T5, cross-attention) architectures.

  • BERT Base: 12 layers, 12 heads, 110M parameters.
  • BERT Large: 24 layers, 16 heads, 340M parameters.
  • Distillation: TinyBERT and DistilBERT compress models up to 9x faster while retaining 95%+ performance.
