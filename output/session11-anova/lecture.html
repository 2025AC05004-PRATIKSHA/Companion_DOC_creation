Session 11: Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)

Compare multiple group means simultaneously using variance decomposition and F-tests.

1. The ANOVA Concept

ANOVA splits total variation into Treatment variation (between groups) and Error variation (within groups).

\[ F = \frac{\text{MSTR}}{\text{MSE}} = \frac{\text{Between-Group Variance}}{\text{Within-Group Variance}} \]
