01 · Complex Models

Multiple \& Quadratic Regression

Multiple regression fits target outcomes across several explanatory inputs. Non-linear models add quadratic or log terms to model diminishing returns.

$$ Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_1 + \beta_2 X_2^2 + \epsilon $$

⚡ Quadratic Curve Fit Simulator

Adjust linear parameter $b$ and quadratic parameter $c$ to see how curvature fits non-linear data trends.

02 · Time Series Breakdown

Decomposition into 4 Components

Every time series combines long-term Secular Trend ($T_t$), periodic Seasonality ($S_t$), economic Cycles ($C_t$), and random Irregular noise ($I_t$).

$$ \text{Additive: } y_t = T_t + S_t + C_t + I_t \quad \Big| \quad \text{Multiplicative: } y_t = T_t \times S_t \times C_t \times I_t $$

⚡ Time Series Synthesizer

Toggle components on and off to observe how trend, seasonal waves, and random noise combine.

03 · Data Stability

Stationarity \& Split-Half Mean Test

Stationary series maintain constant mean and variance over time. If means differ across halves, apply first differencing $w_t = y_t - y_{t-1}$.

⚡ Differencing \& Stationarity Tester

Toggle raw non-stationary series versus differenced series to verify constant mean stability.

04 · Summary Card

Session 12 Pocket Cheat-Sheet

Quadratic Models

$Y = a + bX + cX^2$. Models curved non-linear patterns.

Decomposition

$y_t = T_t + S_t + C_t + I_t$ (Additive) or $T_t \times S_t \times C_t \times I_t$ (Multiplicative).

First Difference

$w_t = y_t - y_{t-1}$. Removes linear trends to achieve stationarity.
