4. BERT Pre-training & Specialized Variants

BERT Architectures
  • BERT Base: 12 layers, 768 hidden units, 12 heads (110M params).
  • BERT Large: 24 layers, 1024 hidden units, 16 heads (340M params).
  • patentBERT / docBERT: Domain-specific fine-tuning for patents and documents.
  • TinyBERT: Compressed student model distilled for fast edge inference.
