ISM Session 13

Time Series Forecasting Methods

Prepared for Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP), BITS Pilani.

Master classic smoothing methods: Simple \& Weighted Moving Averages, Simple Exponential Smoothing ($\alpha$), and Holt's Double Exponential Smoothing for trending data.

1. Moving Averages

Smooth stationary noise by taking simple or weighted averages over past $k$ periods.

2. Exponential Smoothing

Assign exponentially decaying weights to historical errors via constant $\alpha$.

3. Holt's Double Exp

Track both level and slope trend simultaneously to eliminate forecast lag.

01 · Equal & Weighted Averages

Moving Averages (SMA \& WMA)

SMA averages $k$ recent observations equally. WMA places higher weights on more recent observations to respond faster to shifts.

$$ F_{52} = \frac{3 y_{51} + 2 y_{50} + 1 y_{49}}{6} $$

⚡ WMA Weight Adjuster

Adjust the latest month weight $w_1$ to see how WMA shifts forecast responsiveness.

02 · Recursive Updating

Simple Exponential Smoothing

Exponential smoothing updates next period's forecast by adding a fraction $\alpha$ of the current forecast error: $F_{t+1} = F_t + \alpha(y_t - F_t)$.

$$ F_{t+1} = \alpha y_t + (1-\alpha) F_t $$

⚡ $\alpha$ Sensitivity Simulator

Slide smoothing constant $\alpha$ from $0.05$ (heavy smoothing) to $0.95$ (fast tracking).

03 · Trend Tracking

Holt's Double Exponential Smoothing

Holt's method estimates both current level $L_t$ and trend velocity $T_t$, forecasting $h$ steps ahead via $\hat{y}_{t+h} = L_t + h T_t$.

⚡ Holt's Trend Tracker

Adjust trend growth velocity $T$ to observe how Holt's forecast projects ahead without lagging.

04 · Summary Card

Session 13 Pocket Cheat-Sheet

SMA / WMA

Averages recent $k$ points. Best for stationary data without trends.

Single Exp ($\alpha$)

$F_{t+1} = F_t + \alpha(y_t - F_t)$. Higher $\alpha$ reacts faster to noise.

Holt's Double Exp

Tracks level + trend. Prevents forecast lag on rising/falling data.
